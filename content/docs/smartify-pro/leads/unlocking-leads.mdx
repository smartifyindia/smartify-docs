---
title: "Unlocking Leads"
description: "How to reveal lead contact details and manage the resulting account change."
---

All leads start **locked** — you can see basic information (name, city, interests) but contact details (phone, email) are hidden. Unlocking a lead changes the lead record and reveals the full contact information. It does **not** spend credits.

## How to unlock a lead

<Steps>
<Step>
### Find the lead

Browse the Leads list or pipeline view and find a lead that matches your service area and expertise.
</Step>
<Step>
### Click Unlock

Click the **Unlock** button on the lead card. Review the lead’s city, interests, and source before continuing.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm

Click **Confirm** to change the lead record and reveal the lead's phone number and email.
</Step>
<Step>
### Contact the customer

Reach out to the customer and move the lead to **Contacted** status in your pipeline.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
Unlocking changes account data and reveals customer contact details. Make sure you intend to access and follow up with the lead before confirming.
</Callout>
