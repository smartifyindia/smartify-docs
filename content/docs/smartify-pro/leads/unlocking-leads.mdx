All leads start **locked** — you can see basic information (name, city, interests) but contact details (phone, email) are hidden. Unlocking a lead costs **1 credit** and reveals the full contact information.

## How to unlock a lead

<Steps>
<Step>
### Find the lead

Browse the Leads list or pipeline view and find a lead that matches your service area and expertise.
</Step>
<Step>
### Click Unlock

Click the **Unlock** button on the lead card. A confirmation dialog shows:
- The credit cost (1 credit)
- Your current credit balance
- Your remaining balance after unlocking
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm

Click **Confirm** to spend the credit. The lead's phone number and email become visible immediately.
</Step>
<Step>
### Contact the customer

Reach out to the customer and move the lead to **Contacted** status in your pipeline.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
Unlocking is **irreversible** — credits are not refunded if the lead doesn't convert. Review the lead's city, interests, and source before unlocking.
</Callout>

## Ways to earn credits

- **Monthly subscription refill** — your plan includes **100 credits per month**
- **Performance bonus** — earn **20 bonus credits** for converting leads
- **Referral program** — [share unconverted leads](../earn-credits) to earn credits when customers verify
- **Free trial** — new accounts get **2 free leads** with full details unlocked

## Your credit balance

Your current credit balance is visible on:
- The Leads page (top-right badge)
- The [Subscriptions](../billing) page
- The [Locations](../locations) page

## Location subscription costs

Credits are also used to subscribe to cities for receiving leads. The monthly cost depends on the demand level:

| Demand Level | Example Cities | Monthly Credit Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Uncovered | Cities with no active installer | Free |
| Low | Kolkata, Jaipur, Lucknow | 10 credits |
| Medium | Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai | 45 credits |
| High | Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore | 120 credits |

See [Locations](../locations) for more details on subscribing to cities.
