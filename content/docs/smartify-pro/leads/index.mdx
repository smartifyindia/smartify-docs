Smartify delivers qualified smart home leads from its customer portals directly to your dashboard. Leads are matched to your service locations and automation categories.

## Accessing leads

Navigate to **Leads** in the sidebar. If you have an active subscription with location coverage, you'll see your lead dashboard.

<Callout>
Leads require an active subscription and at least one subscribed location. If you haven't set up locations yet, visit [Locations](../locations) to subscribe to cities.
</Callout>

## Viewing leads

Switch between two views using the toggle at the top:

### List View

Each lead appears as a card showing:
- Customer name
- City
- Status badge (New, Unlocked, Contacted, etc.)
- Automation interests (Lighting, Security, HVAC, etc.)
- Lead source

### Pipeline View

A kanban-style board with columns for each stage:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Fresh lead, not yet unlocked |
| **Unlocked** | Contact details revealed (credits spent) |
| **Contacted** | You've reached out to the customer |
| **Quoted** | Estimate sent to the customer |
| **Won** | Deal closed |
| **Lost** | Customer chose another provider |

Drag leads between columns to update their status, or use the status dropdown on each card.

## Filtering leads

Use the status filter to show only leads in a specific stage (e.g., show only New leads, or only Contacted leads).

## Lead details

Click any lead card to open the detail modal with:
- Full contact information (if unlocked)
- Lead source and city
- Automation interests and categories
- Activity history
