title: "AI Estimate Mode"
description: "Build smart home estimates by chatting with the AI — describe what your customer needs and the system builds the estimate for you."
---

AI Estimate Mode lets you create estimates through a conversational interface instead of the step-by-step wizard. Describe what your customer needs in plain language, and the AI builds out rooms and devices in real time.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start a new AI estimate

From the Estimates page, click **New Estimate** and select **AI Mode**. You'll see a split-screen layout:
- **Left panel:** Live estimate preview that updates as the AI works
- **Right panel:** Chat interface
</Step>
<Step>
### Describe the project

Tell the AI about the customer's home and requirements. For example:

*"3BHK apartment in Mumbai, 1200 sqft, ready to move in. Customer wants full lighting automation, curtain control in the living room and master bedroom, and AC control in all rooms."*
</Step>
<Step>
### Refine through conversation

The AI builds the estimate and you can refine it conversationally:
- *"Add a motion sensor in the hallway"*
- *"Switch to KNX protocol for the living room"*
- *"Remove the IR blaster from the kitchen"*
- *"What would you recommend for the balcony?"*

The left panel updates in real time as you chat.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and generate report

Once satisfied, click **Generate Report** to produce the full itemised estimate with pricing, GST, and PDF.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
AI Mode is best for quick estimates or when you want to explore options conversationally. For precise room-by-room control with floor plan mapping, use the [standard 3-step wizard](./creating-an-estimate).
</Callout>
