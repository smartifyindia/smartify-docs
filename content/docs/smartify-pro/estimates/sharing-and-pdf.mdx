---
title: "Sharing & PDF"
description: "Download, share, and track your smart home estimates — PDF generation, WhatsApp sharing, and status management."
---

Once your estimate report is ready, you can share it with your client and track its progress.

## Download the PDF

From the report page, click **Download PDF** to save a professional estimate document with:
- Cover page with your company branding
- Room-by-room device summary
- Floor plan layout
- Full device catalog with pricing
- GST breakdown and grand total

## Share with your client

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Share

On the report page, click the **Share** button to open the sharing dialog.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose sharing method

- **WhatsApp:** Sends the estimate PDF directly to the client's WhatsApp number
- **Shareable link:** Generates a link you can send via any channel
</Step>
</Steps>

## Track estimate status

After sharing, update the estimate status to keep your pipeline organised:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Mark as Sent

When you share the estimate, it automatically moves to **Sent** status.
</Step>
<Step>
### Mark as Accepted or Rejected

Use the **Mark As** dropdown on the report page to update the status when the client responds. A confirmation dialog ensures you don't change status accidentally.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
You can also **lock a lead** from the report page if the estimate contains a majority of Smartify products. Locking reserves the customer for your account for **90 days** — no other Smartify dealer can share an estimate with that client during this period.
</Callout>

## Print the estimate

Click **Print** on the report page to send the estimate directly to your printer.
