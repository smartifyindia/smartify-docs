---
title: "Creating an Estimate"
description: "Step-by-step guide to creating a smart home estimate using the 3-step wizard — property details, room configuration, and report generation."
---

The estimate wizard has three steps: **Property Details → Room Configuration → Report**.

## Step 1 — Property Details

<Steps>
<Step>
### Select or create a contact

Search for an existing customer or type a new name to create a contact on the fly.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter property information

- **Property type:** 1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK, Villa, Penthouse, Commercial, etc.
- **Carpet area:** Total area in square feet
- **Construction status:** Under Construction or Ready to Move
</Step>
<Step>
### Upload a floor plan

Upload a JPG or PNG image of the floor plan. This is used in the next step to map rooms visually.

<Callout>
Enable **AI Floor Plan Analysis** to have the system automatically detect rooms from the uploaded image. You can adjust the detected rooms manually afterward.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step>
### Select automation categories

Check the categories to include in this estimate (e.g., Smart Home, Security, Smart Lights, Curtain Rollers, Climate Control, Energy Management, and more). These match the categories you selected during [onboarding](../getting-started#step-5--select-automation-categories).
</Step>
<Step>
### Set preferences (optional)

Override your default technology preferences for this specific estimate — wiring type, protocol, and brand preferences.
</Step>
</Steps>

Click **Proceed** to move to room configuration, or **Save as Draft** to continue later.

## Step 2 — Room Configuration

This is the most detailed step. You'll see your floor plan as an interactive map with a device panel.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Draw rooms on the floor plan

Click and draw polygons on the floor plan to define each room. Label each room (e.g., "Master Bedroom", "Kitchen", "Living Room").

<Callout>
If you enabled AI Floor Plan Analysis, rooms are pre-detected. You can rename, resize, or add new rooms as needed.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step>
### Add devices to each room

Select a room, then browse or search the product catalog to add devices. For each device, set:
- **Quantity** — how many units for this room
- **Placement** — where in the room (optional)
</Step>
<Step>
### Use the AI Co-Pilot (optional)

Toggle the **Co-Pilot** sidebar for AI-powered device recommendations based on the room type and your selected automation categories. Click **Add to estimate** on any suggestion to include it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review the device list

Each room shows a table of all assigned devices with quantities. You can edit or remove devices at any time.
</Step>
</Steps>

Click **Generate Report** when all rooms are configured.

## Step 3 — Report

The system calculates pricing and generates a comprehensive report:

- **Automation Score** — ratings across five dimensions (Security &amp; Safety, Comfort &amp; Climate, Lighting &amp; Atmosphere, Energy Efficiency, Smart Living) showing how complete the automation setup is, with an **Improve** button for each category that suggests specific devices to raise the score
- **AI Recommendations** — additional device suggestions you might have missed, with one-click add
- **Itemised Estimate Table** — every device listed with unit price, quantity, GST, discounts, and line totals
- **Summary** — subtotal, total GST, and grand total

You can **edit quantities and prices inline** directly in the table before sharing with the client.

<Callout>
Click **Edit** to go back to Step 1 and make changes. The report regenerates automatically when you return.
</Callout>
