---
title: "Earn Credits"
description: "Share unused leads through the referral program to earn credits for unlocking new leads."
---

The **Earn Credits** program lets you turn unconverted leads into credits. If you have a lead you couldn't close, share it back to the platform — if the customer verifies their interest, you earn credits.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Earn Credits

Click **Earn Credits** in the sidebar or navigate from the Leads page.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fill in the lead details

Enter the customer's information:
- **Name**
- **Phone number**
- **City** (with autocomplete)
- **Reason you couldn't close** (e.g., budget, timing, chose another provider)
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm consent

Confirm that the customer is aware their information is being shared. This is a required step.
</Step>
<Step>
### Customer verification

The system sends the customer a WhatsApp message to verify their interest in smart home automation. This happens automatically.
</Step>
<Step>
### Earn credits

If the customer verifies, credits are added to your balance. You can see the status of all your referrals in the **Past Referrals** table on the same page.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Referral statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | WhatsApp verification sent, waiting for customer response |
| **Verified** | Customer confirmed — credits awarded |
| **Denied** | Customer did not verify or declined |

<Callout>
Only share leads where the customer genuinely expressed interest in smart home automation. Sharing unrelated contacts wastes your time and may result in denied referrals.
</Callout>
