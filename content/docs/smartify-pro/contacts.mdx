title: "Contacts"
description: "Manage your customer database — add contacts, import via CSV, and track activity history."
---

The Contacts section is your customer database. Every client you create an estimate for is stored here with their property details and activity history.

## Viewing contacts

Navigate to **Contacts** in the sidebar to see your full contact list. Use the search bar to find contacts by name, phone, or email.

## Adding a contact

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Add Contact

Click the **Add Contact** button at the top of the contacts list.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fill in details

Enter the customer's information:
- Name, email, phone number
- Property type (1BHK, Villa, etc.)
- Carpet area
- Construction status
- Address
</Step>
<Step>
### Save

Click **Save** to add the contact to your database.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Importing contacts via CSV

<Steps>
<Step>
### Go to Upload

Navigate to **Contacts → Upload** or click the import button on the contacts list.
</Step>
<Step>
### Upload your CSV file

Select a CSV file from your computer.
</Step>
<Step>
### Map columns

The system shows a column mapping interface. Match each column in your CSV to the corresponding contact field (name, phone, email, etc.).
</Step>
<Step>
### Import

Confirm the mapping and import. Contacts are added to your database.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Contact details

Click any contact to open their detail view:

- **Contact information** — name, email, phone, property details, address. Click **Edit** to update.
- **Profile photo** — upload or change the contact's photo.
- **Activity timeline** — a chronological feed of all interactions: estimates created, notes added, status changes, and calls logged.
