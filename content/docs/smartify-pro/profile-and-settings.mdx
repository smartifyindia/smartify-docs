title: "Profile & Settings"

---

The Profile page consolidates all your account settings. Navigate to it from the sidebar or your avatar menu.

## Personal profile

Update your name, email, phone number, and profile photo. Click the photo to upload a new image.

## Company details

- **Company name**
- **Contact email and phone** — verify these via OTP for lead notifications
- **GSTIN** — required for GST invoices
- **Address** — street, city, state, and pin code

## Custom branding

<Callout>
Custom branding is available on supported subscription plans. When enabled, your branding appears on estimates, PDFs, and client-facing pages.
</Callout>

Customise how your business appears to clients:
- **Brand name** — your company name on estimates
- **Brand logo** — upload your company logo
- **Favicon** — custom browser tab icon
- **Email address** — outbound email sender address
- **Email display name** — name shown in client emails
- **Primary colour** — your brand colour for estimates and the platform theme

## Notification preferences

Control how you receive alerts:

### Global toggles
- **Email notifications** — on/off
- **WhatsApp notifications** — on/off

### Per-event preferences

Choose email, WhatsApp, or both for each event type:
- Lead assigned to you
- Lead reallocated
- Shared lead — customer verified
- Shared lead — picked up by another installer
- Shared lead — converted
- Shared lead — expired

## Auto follow-up

Enable automatic follow-up messages to customers after you share an estimate:
- **WhatsApp follow-ups** — automated reminders via WhatsApp
- **SMS follow-ups** — automated reminders via SMS

## Technology preferences

Set your default preferences for new estimates:
- **Wiring type:** Wireless, Wired, or Both
- **Protocols:** Zigbee, Z-Wave, KNX, WiFi, etc.
- **Brand preferences**
- **Expert installation:** Toggle whether you provide professional installation services

## Other settings

- **Service categories** — update the automation categories your business handles
- **Service locations** — update cities you serve
- **Certifications** — add or remove professional certifications
- **Experience &amp; team size** — update your business profile

<Callout>
Click the floating **Save** button that appears when you have unsaved changes. All sections are saved together.
</Callout>
