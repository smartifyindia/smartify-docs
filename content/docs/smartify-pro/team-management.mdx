title: "Team Management"
description: "Invite team members, assign roles, and manage access to your Smartify Pro organization."
---

Invite your colleagues to your Smartify Pro organization so they can create estimates, manage contacts, and access leads.

## Inviting a team member

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Team Management

Click **Manage Team** from the dashboard header or navigate to the Team page.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter their email

Type the email address of the person you want to invite.
</Step>
<Step>
### Select a role

Choose the appropriate role:

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Create and manage own estimates, view contacts and leads |
| **Admin** | Everything Members can do, plus manage team members and organization settings |
| **Owner** | Full control including billing, plan management, and transferring ownership |

</Step>
<Step>
### Send invitation

Click **Invite**. The person receives an email invitation to join your organization.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Managing team members

The **People with access** list shows all current team members with their name, email, and role.

### Changing roles

Use the role dropdown next to any team member to change their role. Admins can promote Members to Admin, but cannot change the Owner's role.

### Removing members

Click the remove icon next to a team member to revoke their access. They will no longer be able to access the organization's data.

<Callout>
Only Owners and Admins can invite or remove team members. Members can view the team list but cannot make changes.
</Callout>
