title: "Inventory"
description: "Browse the product catalog, manage custom devices, and track stock levels."
---

The Inventory section gives you access to the full Smartify product catalog plus any custom products you've added.

## Browsing products

Navigate to **Inventory** in the sidebar. Use the tabs to switch between:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All Products** | Combined view of catalog and custom products |
| **My Products** | Only your custom/user-added products |
| **Catalog** | The global Smartify product catalog |

### Filtering

Use the filter panel to narrow results by:
- **Category** — Switches, Panels, Sensors, Gateways, etc.
- **Brand** — Smartify or third-party brands
- **Price range** — minimum and maximum price
- **Protocol** — Zigbee, WiFi, KNX, etc.

## Product details

Click any product to open its detail page:
- Product image gallery
- Category, protocol, and SKU
- Pricing: MRP, selling price, and GST rate
- Overview and use cases
- Installation guide (time, requirements, steps)
- Technical specifications
- Compatible products
- FAQ

## Adding custom products

If a device isn't in the Smartify catalog, you can add it as a custom product:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Import Devices

Click the **Import** button on the Inventory page.
</Step>
<Step>
### Upload a CSV

Prepare a CSV with product details (name, category, price, GST rate, etc.) and upload it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and confirm

The system previews the imported products. Confirm to add them to your **My Products** list.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Updating stock

Click **Update Stock** to adjust current stock levels for your products. This helps you track which devices you have on hand.

<Callout>
Catalog products are maintained by Smartify and updated automatically. Pricing syncs periodically — the last sync time is shown at the top of the page.
</Callout>
