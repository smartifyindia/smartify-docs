Locations determine which cities you receive leads from. Subscribe to cities where you offer installation services to start receiving matched customer leads.

## Your active locations

At the top of the Locations page, you'll see all your currently subscribed cities with:
- **Demand level** indicator (how much customer interest exists)
- **Monthly credit cost** for that city
- **Unsubscribe** button to stop receiving leads from that city

## Finding new locations

### Opportunities tab

Shows cities that are **uncovered** (no installer serving them) or have **low competition**. Uncovered cities appear first — these are free to subscribe to and give you exclusive access to leads in that area.

### Browse All tab

Search and filter all available cities:
- **Search** by city name or state
- **Filter by demand:** Uncovered, Low, Medium, or High

Each city card shows the demand level, number of active partners, and a **Subscribe** button.

## Demand levels and costs

| Demand | What it means | Monthly credit cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Uncovered** | No active installer in this city | Free |
| **Low** | Few customers, few installers | Low |
| **Medium** | Moderate demand and competition | Medium |
| **High** | High demand, multiple installers | High |

<Callout>
Start with uncovered cities for free leads, then expand to higher-demand areas as your credit balance grows.
</Callout>

## Subscribing to a city

Click **Subscribe** on any city card. The subscription starts immediately and you'll begin receiving leads from that city. The monthly credit cost is deducted automatically.

## Unsubscribing

Click **Unsubscribe** on any active location to stop receiving leads from that city. You won't be charged credits for that city in the next cycle.
