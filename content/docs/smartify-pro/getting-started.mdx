This guide walks you through the full setup process — from creating your account to completing onboarding and reaching your dashboard.

## Create your account

<Steps>
<Step>
### Sign up

Open [**pro.smartify.in**](https://pro.smartify.in) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your:
- **Name**
- **Email address**
- **Password** (minimum 5 characters, must include an uppercase letter, a number, and a special character)

You can also sign up with your **Google account** for a faster registration.
</Step>
<Step>
### Verify your email

You'll receive a 6-digit OTP on your email. Enter it on the verification screen to activate your account. If you don't see it, wait 30 seconds and click **Resend**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Log in

After verification, log in with your email and password (or Google). You'll be taken to the onboarding wizard.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Forgot your password?

Click **Forgot Password** on the login screen. Enter your email address and follow the reset instructions sent to your inbox.

## Onboarding wizard

After your first login, the onboarding wizard sets up your business. A progress bar tracks your completion across 6 steps.

<Callout>
You can skip onboarding at any step by clicking **Try for Free**. You'll get a 7-day trial with up to 10 estimates and 2 free leads. Complete onboarding later from your profile to unlock the full platform.
</Callout>

### Step 1 — Verify your phone number

Enter your mobile number (+91). You'll receive a 6-digit OTP via WhatsApp to verify.

### Step 2 — Choose your path

Select one of two options:

- **"Yes, I'm a business owner"** — create a new organization ([continue below](#step-3--select-partnership-type))
- **"No, joining an existing business"** — join a colleague's organization ([skip to Joining a Team](#joining-an-existing-team))

### Step 3 — Select partnership type

Choose the plan that fits your business:

| | Franchisee Partner | Multi-brand Partner |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual fee** | ₹30,000 | ₹60,000 |
| **Qualification fee** | ₹2,500 | ₹2,500 |
| **Key benefit** | Minimum Assured Business (2 locations) | 100% ROI Guarantee |
| **Includes** | Demo kit + training, free uncovered locations | Brand flexibility, ROI credits if targets not met |
| **Best for** | Dedicated Smartify installers | Multi-brand integrators |

If you select Multi-brand, you'll also choose which brands you work with.

### Step 4 — Enter business details

Fill in your company information:
- Company name
- Contact phone
- GST number
- Business address
- Years of experience
- Team size

### Step 5 — Select automation categories

Choose the categories your business specialises in. This determines which products appear in your estimates and which leads you receive:

- Smart Home
- Security
- Smart Lights
- Curtain Rollers
- Voice Enabled
- Multimedia
- Security System
- Surveillance
- Accident Protection
- Climate Control
- Appliance Automation
- Energy Management
- Gardening &amp; Outdoor

### Step 6 — Technology preferences

Set your default technology stack (all fields are optional — you can update these later):

- **Wiring type:** Wireless or Wired
- **Wireless protocols:** Zigbee, Z-Wave, Thread, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi
- **Wired protocols:** KNX
- **Brands:** Smartify, Havells, Schneider Electric, Legrand, Philips Hue, Wipro Lighting, Syska, Goldmedal, Anchor, Honeywell, Oakter, Tuya, Sonoff, and more
- **Expert installation:** Toggle whether you provide professional installation services

### Step 7 — Select service locations

Pick the cities where you offer installation services. Each city shows a demand indicator so you can see where customer interest is highest, along with the monthly credit cost.

### Step 8 — Review and pay

Review all your settings on the summary screen. Apply a coupon code if you have one, then pay the qualification fee (₹2,500) via Razorpay (UPI, card, or net banking) to complete onboarding.

<Callout>
You can update all of these settings later from [Profile &amp; Settings](./profile-and-settings).
</Callout>

## Joining an existing team

If your company already has a Smartify Pro account, you can join their organization instead of creating a new one.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Verify your phone number

Enter your mobile number and verify it via WhatsApp OTP (same as Step 1 above).
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose "Joining an existing business"

Select this option on the account type screen.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter organization ID

Ask your team owner or admin for the organization ID and enter it. The system validates the ID and sends a join request.
</Step>
<Step>
### Wait for approval

You'll see a **Pending Approval** screen while the organization owner reviews your request. You can:
- Click **Check Status** to refresh
- Click **Cancel Request** to withdraw
- Click **Start a new account** to create your own organization instead
</Step>
<Step>
### Access granted

Once approved, you'll have access to the organization's dashboard, estimates, contacts, and other features based on your assigned role (Member or Admin).
</Step>
</Steps>
