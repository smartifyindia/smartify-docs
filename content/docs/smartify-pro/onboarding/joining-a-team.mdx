---
title: "Join an Existing Team"
description: "How to join a colleague's existing Smartify Pro organization."
---

If your company already has a Smartify Pro account, you can join their organization instead of creating a new one.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Verify your phone number

Enter your mobile number (+91) and verify it via WhatsApp OTP.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose account type

Select **"No, joining an existing business"**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter organization ID

Ask your team owner or admin for the organization ID and enter it. The system validates the ID and sends a join request to the account owner.
</Step>
<Step>
### Wait for approval

Your request goes to the organization owner for review. You'll see a **Pending Approval** screen while waiting. You can:
- Click **Check Status** to refresh
- Click **Cancel Request** to withdraw your request
- Click **Start a new account** to create your own organization instead
</Step>
<Step>
### Access granted

Once approved, you'll have access to the organization's dashboard, estimates, contacts, and other features based on your assigned role (Member or Admin).
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
If your request is taking too long, contact your organization's owner directly. They can find the organization ID on the [Team Management](../team-management) page.
</Callout>
