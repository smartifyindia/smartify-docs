---
title: "New Business Setup"
description: "Step-by-step guide to registering a new business on Smartify Pro — partnership type, company details, categories, brands, and service locations."
---

Follow these steps to set up your business on Smartify Pro. A progress bar tracks your completion across 6 steps.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Verify your phone number

Enter your mobile number (+91) and verify it with the 6-digit OTP sent via WhatsApp.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose account type

Select **"Yes, I'm a business owner"** to create a new organization.
</Step>
<Step>
### Select partnership type

Choose between:

| | Franchisee Partner | Multi-brand Partner |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual fee** | ₹30,000 | ₹60,000 |
| **Qualification fee** | ₹2,500 | ₹2,500 |
| **Key benefit** | Minimum Assured Business (2 locations) | 100% ROI Guarantee |
| **Includes** | Demo kit + training, free uncovered locations | Brand flexibility, ROI credits if targets not met |
| **Best for** | Dedicated Smartify installers | Multi-brand integrators |

If you select Multi-brand, you'll also choose which brands you work with.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter business details

Fill in your company information:
- Company name
- Contact phone
- GST number
- Business address
- Years of experience
- Team size
</Step>
<Step>
### Select automation categories

Choose the categories your business specialises in. This determines which products appear in your estimates and which leads you receive:
- Smart Home
- Security
- Smart Lights
- Curtain Rollers
- Voice Enabled
- Multimedia
- Security System
- Surveillance
- Accident Protection
- Climate Control
- Appliance Automation
- Energy Management
- Gardening &amp; Outdoor
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose technology preferences

Set your default technology stack (all fields are optional):
- **Wiring type:** Wireless or Wired
- **Wireless protocols:** Zigbee, Z-Wave, Thread, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi
- **Wired protocols:** KNX
- **Brands:** Smartify, Havells, Schneider Electric, Legrand, Philips Hue, Wipro Lighting, Syska, Goldmedal, Anchor, Honeywell, Oakter, Tuya, Sonoff, and more
- **Expert installation:** Toggle whether you provide professional installation services
</Step>
<Step>
### Select service locations

Pick the cities where you offer installation services. Each city shows a demand indicator so you can see where customer interest is highest, along with the monthly credit cost.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and pay

Review all your settings on the summary screen. Apply a coupon code if you have one, then pay the qualification fee (₹2,500) via Razorpay (UPI, card, or net banking) to complete onboarding.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
You can update all of these settings later from [Profile &amp; Settings](../profile-and-settings).
</Callout>
