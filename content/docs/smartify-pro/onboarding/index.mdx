After your first login, the onboarding wizard guides you through setting up your account. Your path depends on whether you're starting a new business or joining an existing one.

For a complete walkthrough of the entire setup process (signup through onboarding), see [Getting Started](../getting-started#onboarding-wizard).

  </Card>
  <Card href="/docs/smartify-pro/onboarding/joining-a-team" title="Join an Existing Team">

  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout>
You can skip onboarding by clicking **Try for Free** at any step. You'll get a 7-day trial with up to 10 estimates and 2 free leads. Complete onboarding later to unlock the full platform.
</Callout>
