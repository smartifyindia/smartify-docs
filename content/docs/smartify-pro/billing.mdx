The Billing page manages your subscription plan, optional lead boost campaigns, and all payment records.

## Partnership plans

Smartify Pro offers two partnership tiers:

| | Franchisee Partner | Multi-brand Partner |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual fee** | ₹30,000 | ₹60,000 |
| **Qualification fee** | ₹2,500 | ₹2,500 |
| **Key benefit** | Minimum Assured Business (MAB) — guaranteed leads in 2 locations | 100% ROI Guarantee — free extension or credits if targets aren't met |
| **Includes** | Demo kit + training, free uncovered locations | Brand flexibility across multiple manufacturers |
| **Best for** | Dedicated Smartify installers | Multi-brand integrators |

Both plans include **100 credits per month** and **20 bonus credits** for lead conversions.

Your current plan is shown with a status badge, validity date, and options to **Renew** or **Cancel**.

### Applying a coupon

Enter a coupon code in the input field next to your plan and click **Apply** to get a discount on your subscription.

### Upgrading or renewing

Click **Upgrade** or **Renew** to proceed to payment via Razorpay. You can pay via UPI, credit/debit card, or net banking.

## Boost Leads

Boost Leads runs targeted Google Ads campaigns for your city and delivers the resulting leads directly to your dashboard.

| Monthly spend | Expected leads |
|---|---|
| ₹5,000 | 7–12 |
| ₹10,000 | 15–25 |
| ₹15,000 | 20–35 |
| ₹20,000 | 30–50 |
| ₹25,000 | 35–60 |
| ₹30,000 | 45–75 |

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose a tier

Select the monthly spend level that matches your lead volume needs.
</Step>
<Step>
### Subscribe

Click **Subscribe** to set up a recurring monthly payment via Razorpay.
</Step>
<Step>
### Manage

Once active, you can **Change Plan** (switch tiers) or **Cancel Boost** at any time. Your current tier, expected lead count, and next billing date are displayed.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Payment history

A table at the bottom of the Billing page shows all past payments with:
- Date
- Amount
- Payment type (subscription, boost, credits)
- Status
- **Download Invoice** — PDF receipt for each payment
- **GST Invoice** — for tax filing purposes
