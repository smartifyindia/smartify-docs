## Description

An 8-button Zigbee touch panel with a dedicated fan control channel for ceiling fans up to 120W, combining multi-circuit load control with integrated fan management in a single wall panel.

Each of the 8 buttons is independently configurable for toggle or momentary operation. The fan channel operates separately for speed and on/off control. Available in white or black glass with silver, gold, or black bezel.

## Specifications

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|

| Max Switching | 10A per button channel |
| Recommended Load | Up to 4A per button channel; fan up to 120W |
| Installation | Wall-mounted |
| Compatibility | Zigbee automation platforms |


## Installation

### Requirements
- Standard flush box compatible with panel dimensions
- Live, Neutral, Load wires
- Zigbee coordinator within range

<Steps>
<Step>
Switch off the circuit breaker.
</Step>
<Step>
Mount the backplate/box to the wall.
</Step>
<Step>
Connect wiring per the channel labels on the panel.
</Step>
<Step>
Snap or screw the panel into position.
</Step>
<Step>
Restore power and pair with your Zigbee coordinator.
</Step>
<Step>
Configure scenes and buttons in the Smartify or Home Assistant UI.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Notes
- Touch panels must be grounded to avoid false triggers.
- Ensure the panel is flush against the wall for reliable capacitive touch detection.
