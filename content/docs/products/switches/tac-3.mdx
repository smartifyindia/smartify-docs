## Description

A 3-button Zigbee smart switch with independent load control or scene triggering per button. Backlit with customizable icons. Available in PP or anti-glare glass in black, white, or gold.

## Installation

### Requirements
- Standard 2-module or 4-module flush box
- Live, Neutral, and Load wires available at the switch location
- Zigbee coordinator (gateway) within range

<Steps>
<Step>
**Turn off the circuit breaker** for the circuit you're working on.
</Step>
<Step>
Remove the existing switch plate and pull out the wires.
</Step>
<Step>
Connect **Live (L)** → L terminal, **Neutral (N)** → N terminal, **Load** → the load terminal(s).
</Step>
<Step>
Tuck the module into the flush box and fit the TAC switch plate.
</Step>
<Step>
Restore power. The LED indicator will blink — the device is ready to pair.
</Step>
<Step>
Open your Zigbee coordinator app (Home Assistant, SmartThings, etc.) and put it in pairing mode.
</Step>
<Step>
Press and hold the switch for 5 seconds until the LED blinks rapidly — pairing begins.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Wiring Notes
- Neutral wire is **required** for all TAC switches.
- Maximum load per channel: see product specifications.
- For ceiling fans, use the TAC F (fan speed controller variant).
